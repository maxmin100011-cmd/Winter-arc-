Schedule
Each reminder fires once a day, and is skipped when the task is already done.
Reminders are checked while Winter Arc is open — in a tab, a background tab, or an installed window. With no server, a fully closed page can't be woken; if one was due while it was closed, it shows on Home the next time you open the app (within a few hours). Some phone browsers only allow system notifications for installed apps — in-app reminders always work.
